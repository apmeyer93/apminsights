Kit is a budgeting app designed for new and expectant mothers. It helps you track spending, set savings goals, build a future fund for your child, and get personalized financial guidance from Kit Advisor — all in one place.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my budget at the start of a new month?
Tap the Budget tab and select Budget Plan. Use the pencil icons to update any income or expense fields. Your spending tracker resets automatically at the start of each calendar month — previous transactions are filtered out so you start fresh.
How do I add a Future Fund account?
Tap the Future Fund tab (leaf icon) and press the + button in the top right. Select your account type (529, UTMA, Trump Account, or Custodial Roth IRA), give it a nickname, enter your current balance and monthly contribution, and tap Add account. Your 18-year wealth projection will update automatically.
Where is my data stored?
All of your budget, spending, goals, and future fund data is stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. Kit does not sync your financial data to external servers. Your Kit Advisor conversations are sent to our secure proxy to generate AI responses and are not stored after processing.
How do I delete my account and data?
To delete all your data, go to Settings (person icon) and tap Sign out. This clears all locally stored data from your device. To permanently delete your account, email us at apm@apminsightsllc.com and we will process your request within 30 days.
How do I manage or cancel my Kit subscription?
Kit subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. To cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Kit. You can cancel or manage your subscription from there. You will retain access through the end of your current billing period.
Is the financial information in Kit advice I should act on?
Kit and Kit Advisor are designed to help you organize and understand your finances. They do not provide professional financial, tax, or investment advice. For decisions about investment accounts, tax strategy, or estate planning, please consult a licensed financial advisor.
