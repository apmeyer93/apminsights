Privacy Policy

Kit — Budgeting for New & Expectant Mothers

Effective date: July 27, 2026

APM Insights LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Kit mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use Kit.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Name, email address, and baby's name provided during account creation
  • Budget information including income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, and baby-related expenses
  • Spending transactions you log manually
  • Savings goal names, target amounts, and contribution amounts
  • Future fund account names, types, balances, and contribution amounts
  • Messages and prompts you send to Kit Advisor

Information collected automatically:

  • App usage data and crash reports collected through standard iOS frameworks
  • Device type and operating system version

How we store your information

All budget, spending, goals, and future fund data is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data does not leave your device except as described below.

Your name, email address, and Kit Advisor conversation messages are transmitted to our secure server infrastructure (hosted on Railway) solely for the purpose of generating AI responses. Kit Advisor conversation content is not retained after a response is generated.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the Kit app and its features
  • To generate personalized responses from Kit Advisor
  • To generate your Monthly AI Recap
  • To improve the App and fix issues
  • To respond to your support requests

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect or store bank account numbers, credit card numbers, or any financial account credentials
  • We do not connect to your bank accounts or financial institutions
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising purposes

Third-party services

Kit uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic: Powers Kit Advisor AI responses. Messages you send to Kit Advisor are processed by Anthropic's API. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages in-app subscriptions and purchase verification. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Apple: App Store, in-app purchases, and standard iOS data collection as described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Kit is intended for use by adults (parents and expectant parents). Kit is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App may reference information about your child (such as baby's name and age) solely for personalization purposes within the App.

Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored data (budget, spending, goals, future fund) remains on your device until you sign out of the App or uninstall it. Signing out clears all locally stored data.

To request deletion of any account information we may hold, email us at apm@apminsightsllc.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission of data to our servers. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Kit after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
